## 简体中文

### 1. 政策范围

本隐私政策说明你使用 CedarPitch 时，我们如何处理信息。CedarPitch 是一款以设备本地处理为主的吉他调音器，无需注册账户。

### 2. 我们处理的信息

开发者不会通过 CedarPitch 收集姓名、电话号码、通讯录、精确位置、广告标识符、麦克风录音或第三方分析数据。本 App 不包含广告，也不集成第三方追踪 SDK。

如果你主动通过支持邮箱或 App Store 的“App 支持”渠道联系我们，我们会收到你主动提供的邮箱地址、问题描述和附件。这些信息仅用于回复、排查问题、处理投诉或履行法律义务，不会用于广告或用户画像。请不要发送支付卡资料或与问题无关的敏感信息。

### 3. 麦克风与音频处理

在获得你的授权后，CedarPitch 使用麦克风检测吉他音高。音频样本通过 Apple AVFoundation API 在设备内存中实时处理。

CedarPitch：

- 不会把麦克风音频录制成文件；
- 不会在音高分析结束后保留音频；
- 不会将麦克风音频上传到开发者服务器；
- 不会将麦克风音频提供给广告商、分析平台或其他第三方。

### 4. 保存在设备上的数据

以下内容保存在你的设备上：

- A4 基准频率；
- 麦克风灵敏度和调音容差；
- 主题、调音模式和所选调弦；
- 你主动保存的调音记录，最多 200 条。

这些设置和记录通过 iOS UserDefaults 保存在本机。

Keychain 会保存购买权益快照，包括商品标识符、原始交易标识符、到期日期、验证时间和 StoreKit 签名交易表示。高级版权限始终以通过 StoreKit 验证的交易为最终依据。如果你启用了 iCloud 钥匙串，Apple 可能在你的设备之间同步该 Keychain 项；CedarPitch 不会将其发送到开发者运营的服务器。

### 5. App 内购买与 Apple 服务

月订阅和永久买断均由 Apple 通过 StoreKit 处理。CedarPitch 只获取商品信息，以及解锁功能所需的、经过加密验证的交易和权益状态。

开发者不会获得你的完整 Apple 账户凭证或支付卡资料。Apple 按照其自身条款和隐私政策处理 App Store 付款、StoreKit 交易和可选的 iCloud 钥匙串同步。你可以查阅 [Apple 隐私政策](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/)。

### 6. 信息共享与导出

CedarPitch 不会出售个人信息，也不会为广告或追踪目的共享个人信息。目前没有由开发者选定并接收 App 用户数据的第三方。

当你主动导出调音记录时，iOS 会显示系统共享面板，CSV 文件只会发送到你选择的目标。这是由你发起的传输，完成后由该目标的隐私规则约束。

如果开发者未来聘用服务提供商处理用户数据，将要求其提供与本政策相同或同等的隐私保护，并会在处理开始前更新本政策及取得所需同意。

### 7. 数据保留与删除

- 本地设置和调音记录会保留在设备上，直到你主动清除或删除 App；
- 你可以在 CedarPitch 的“工具”页面清空调音记录；
- 删除 App 会移除其 UserDefaults 数据；
- iOS Keychain 项在重装 App 后仍可能保留；
- StoreKit 购买历史由 Apple 保存，并可使用同一 Apple 账户恢复；
- 支持沟通信息只会在回复问题、处理争议或履行法律义务所需的期限内保留，之后会被删除或匿名化。

CedarPitch 没有开发者运营的账户系统或服务端用户数据库，因此不存在可由开发者删除的服务端 App 用户档案。如需删除你主动发送给支持渠道的信息，请使用本政策顶部的联系邮箱提出请求。

### 8. 你的选择与权利

你可以随时前往“系统设置 > 隐私与安全性 > 麦克风”拒绝或撤回麦克风权限。没有麦克风权限时，实时调音无法工作，但仍可查看设置和法律信息。

你可以自行决定是否保存或导出调音记录，并可在 Apple 账户的订阅设置中管理或取消订阅。如果你曾主动联系我们，可以通过本政策顶部的邮箱请求查询、更正或删除你在支持沟通中提供的信息；我们会在适用法律要求的范围内处理。

### 9. 儿童隐私

CedarPitch 并非以收集儿童个人信息为目的，也不会在明知的情况下收集此类信息。本 App 没有账户、广告或社交功能。如果你认为儿童通过支持渠道向我们提供了个人信息，请联系我们删除。

### 10. 安全措施

CedarPitch 坚持数据最小化，并使用 iOS 本地存储保护、Keychain 和 StoreKit 验证。任何设备存储或电子通信方式都无法保证绝对安全，请妥善保护你的设备和 Apple 账户。

当 App 功能、数据处理方式或法律要求发生变化时，本政策可能更新。最新版会在 App 内和公开隐私政策页面提供，并更新页面顶部的生效日期。

## English

### 1. Scope

This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use CedarPitch. CedarPitch is designed as an on-device guitar tuner and does not require an account.

### 2. Information We Handle

The developer does not collect names, phone numbers, contacts, precise location, advertising identifiers, microphone recordings, or third-party analytics data through CedarPitch. The app contains no advertising or third-party tracking SDKs.

If you voluntarily contact us through the support email address or the App Support channel on the App Store, we receive the email address, message, and attachments you choose to provide. We use this information only to reply, troubleshoot, handle complaints, or comply with legal obligations. We do not use it for advertising or profiling. Do not send payment-card details or unrelated sensitive information.

### 3. Microphone and Audio Processing

With your permission, CedarPitch accesses the microphone to detect guitar pitch. Audio samples are processed in real time in device memory using Apple AVFoundation APIs.

CedarPitch does not:

- record microphone audio to a file;
- retain microphone audio after pitch analysis;
- upload microphone audio to a developer-operated server; or
- provide microphone audio to advertisers, analytics platforms, or other third parties.

### 4. Data Stored on Your Device

The following information is stored on your device:

- A4 reference pitch;
- microphone sensitivity and tuning tolerance;
- theme, tuner mode, and selected tuning; and
- up to 200 tuning log entries that you choose to save.

These settings and logs are stored locally using iOS UserDefaults.

Keychain stores a purchase-entitlement snapshot containing the product identifier, original transaction identifier, expiration date, verification time, and signed StoreKit transaction representation. Verified StoreKit transactions are always the authoritative source for Premium access. If iCloud Keychain is enabled, Apple may synchronize this Keychain item across your devices. CedarPitch does not send it to a developer-operated server.

### 5. In-App Purchases and Apple Services

Monthly subscriptions and lifetime purchases are processed by Apple through StoreKit. CedarPitch receives product information and cryptographically verified transaction and entitlement status needed to unlock features.

The developer does not receive your full Apple Account credentials or payment-card details. Apple processes App Store payments, StoreKit transactions, and optional iCloud Keychain synchronization under Apple’s own terms and privacy policy. You can review the [Apple Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/).

### 6. Sharing and Export

CedarPitch does not sell personal information or share it for advertising or tracking. There is currently no developer-selected third party that receives app user data.

When you deliberately export a tuning log, iOS displays the system share sheet and sends the CSV only to the destination you select. This is a user-directed transfer, and the destination’s privacy practices apply after you share it.

If the developer later engages a service provider to process user data, that provider will be required to provide the same or equal privacy protection described in this policy. This policy and any required consent will be updated before such processing begins.

### 7. Retention and Deletion

- Local settings and tuning logs remain on the device until you clear them or remove the app.
- You can delete tuning logs from the Tools screen in CedarPitch.
- Removing the app deletes its UserDefaults data.
- iOS Keychain items may remain after reinstallation.
- StoreKit purchase history is maintained by Apple and can be restored using the same Apple Account.
- Support communications are retained only as long as needed to respond, resolve disputes, or comply with legal obligations, after which they are deleted or anonymized.

CedarPitch has no developer-operated account system or server-side user database, so the developer has no server-stored app user profile to delete. To request deletion of information you voluntarily sent through a support channel, contact the email address at the top of this policy.

### 8. Your Choices and Rights

You can deny or revoke microphone access at any time in iOS Settings > Privacy & Security > Microphone. Without microphone access, live tuning cannot work, but you can still review settings and legal information.

You control whether readings are saved or exported and can manage or cancel subscriptions in your Apple Account settings. If you contacted us voluntarily, you may use the email address at the top of this policy to request access to, correction of, or deletion of information you provided in the support communication. We will respond as required by applicable law.

### 9. Children’s Privacy

CedarPitch is not designed to collect personal information from children and does not knowingly collect such information. The app has no account system, advertising, or social features. If you believe a child sent personal information through a support channel, contact us to request deletion.

### 10. Security

CedarPitch minimizes data use and relies on iOS local-storage protections, Keychain, and StoreKit verification. No method of device storage or electronic communication is absolutely secure, so keep your device and Apple Account protected.

### 11. Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated when app features, data-handling practices, or legal requirements change. The current version will be made available in the app and on the public privacy-policy page with a revised effective date.
